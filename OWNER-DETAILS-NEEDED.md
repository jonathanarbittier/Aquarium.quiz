# Owner details needed before final trust/legal review

TankPicks currently has no owner-supplied public identity or contact channel. Do not invent these details.

Please provide:

- Public owner or business name
- Country/jurisdiction
- Public contact email or contact-page destination
- Mailing address, if legally required for the owner’s operation
- Actual Amazon Associate ID, only after the account is approved
- Confirmation that the required Amazon Associates disclosure wording is acceptable for the enrolled locale

After these are supplied, update `/about/`, `/privacy/`, and `assets/config.js` as appropriate. Affiliate tracking must remain disabled until a valid Associate ID is supplied.
