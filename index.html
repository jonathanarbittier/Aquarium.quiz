How TankPicks works

A practical shortlist, not a substitute for planning

TankPicks checks your answers against manufacturer-published aquarium capacity, water-type compatibility, flow controls, and installation style. Rankings are generated from those specifications and your quiz answers. We do not hands-on test these products.
